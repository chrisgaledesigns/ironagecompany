About The Shop

01 / Company

Iron Age Company specializes in the forming, stamping, and fabrication of restoration quality sheet metal panels and components for the automotive, aircraft, agricultural, and military equipment of yesteryear.

Every panel is built to original gauge or heavier, matched to factory contours, and finished for a correct, driver-quality restoration — from full fenders down to the smallest cage nut.

What Is An SJ / FSJ?

Jeep's SJ platform was part of the "FSJ," or full-size Jeep, lineup. A vehicle qualifies as FSJ when it:

  • Carries the Jeep nameplate and was produced in North America
  • Has a wheelbase between 109 in (2,769 mm) and 132 in (3,353 mm)
  • Has a tread width between 57 in (1,448 mm) and 67 in (1,702 mm)
