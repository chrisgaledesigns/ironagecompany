Ordering

How To Order

We don't have a shopping cart yet — we're focused on developing the product line. Here's how to place an order by email in the meantime.

Placing An Order

4 Steps
Step 1

Send Us An Email

Include the following:

  • First and last name
  • Shipping address (specify business or residential) — Wisconsin residents add 5.5% sales tax
  • Phone number, so the shipping company can find you (optional for continental US, required for foreign orders to complete customs paperwork)
  • The item(s) you'd like to purchase, with item code number if available
  • Quantity
  • Your preferred payment method
  • Any questions you have
Do not send your credit card info. We use PayPal so your personal and private information stays personal and private. If you'd like to pay by credit card, we'll send a PayPal invoice — or you may send a check or money order to Iron Age Company, P.O. Box 689, Chetek, WI 54728. All funds must clear the bank before shipment.
Step 2

We Send A Quote

We reply with a shipping quote, order total, estimated shipping date, and answers to any questions.

Step 3

You Approve The Order

Confirm the quote and we'll get your order into the queue.

Step 4

We Process & Ship

Your order is processed and shipped as soon as possible.
